Battle Rules

Back to game

Choose Battle in the Rules menu. Each team starts with one starred heavy marble in the middle of its row of three. A heavy marble can only step into an adjacent empty hole. Win by getting all ten marbles into your goal.

Radial knockback

→ ↗ →

Adjacent opponents are pushed one hole directly away from the landing hole. Resolve the opponents closest to the direction of travel first, with clockwise order breaking ties. Teammates resist the initial hit, and heavy marbles resist all knockback.

Chain reactions

→ →

If the next hole is occupied, the force carries through the line to the first empty hole. Every marble in the chain moves one hole, including teammates. A heavy marble blocks the chain and can deflect the push.

Edge deflection

↘

When the straight route is blocked or runs off the board, use the shortest chain to an empty hole. Ties favor the outward direction, then the heavy marble’s direction of travel. The game tries to leave other opponents adjacent to the blast in place for their own pushes.
